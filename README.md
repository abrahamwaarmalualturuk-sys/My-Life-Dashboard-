# My Life Dashboard

Fresh rebuild of the personal life-management dashboard.

Includes Dashboard, Today, Tasks, Goals, Calendar, Projects, Notes, Areas, Documents and Settings; persistent browser storage; progress tracking; deadlines; global search; dark mode; JSON export; responsive mobile navigation; and optional Supabase cloud sync.

Starter areas include University & Study, Career & Jobs, Businesses & Organisations, Programming & Digital Skills, Social Media & Content, Finance & Business Planning, Documents & Projects and Personal Development, with SSYDIO and KUOY AGRISOL LTD starter work.
